🤖 How the AI reached this decision

Transparency note on the XAI methodology used.

🌲
XGBoost Classifier

An ensemble of decision trees trained on thousands of historical claims. Each tree votes; the combined probability determines fraud likelihood.

📊
SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)

A game-theory based method that attributes the output to each feature. Positive SHAP = feature increases fraud probability; negative SHAP = feature lowers it.

⚖️
Business Rule Engine

Deterministic rules (e.g., claim-to-premium ratio, sub-claim mismatch) layer on top of the ML model to catch financial inconsistencies.

🎯
Fraud Threshold: 20%

If final risk score ≥ 20%, the claim is flagged as Fraud. This conservative threshold maximises recall to avoid missing real fraud cases.
